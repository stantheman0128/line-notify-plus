Notify+ 隱私權政策

最後更新日期：2026 年 8 月 29 日

本政策說明 Notify+（以下稱「本應用程式」）如何處理您的資料。請在使用前詳細閱讀。

1. 本應用程式做什麼

本應用程式是一個通知增強工具。它會讀取 LINE 應用程式發出的通知，重新整理成更易閱讀的形式（對話串模式、分組模式等）後重新顯示。

2. 我們存取哪些資料

為了提供上述功能，本應用程式需要「通知存取權限」，藉此讀取 LINE 通知的內容，包含：傳送者名稱、訊息文字、聊天室名稱、傳送者頭貼。若您另外選擇啟用實驗性的「LINE 聊天室開啟偵測」，Android 的無障礙服務會讓本應用程式存取 LINE 畫面結構與可見文字；本應用程式只使用目前聊天室名稱與必要畫面狀態來清除對應通知。

3. 資料如何被使用

  • 所有處理皆在您的裝置本機進行。本應用程式不會將收到的通知內容上傳或儲存到開發者的伺服器、自有後端或其他服務。您主動使用快速回覆時，會依下一點將該次回覆交給 LINE。
  • 訊息文字與傳送者資訊會暫存在程序記憶體中，用於即時重組通知；本應用程式不會把完整訊息歷史寫入自己的持久檔案。為了顯示通知，內容會交給 Android 通知系統，並可能依您的通知紀錄設定保留到通知被清除或系統保留期限結束，即使本應用程式程序已結束亦同。
  • 只有在您主動使用「快速回覆」時，本應用程式才會將您輸入的回覆交給 LINE 應用程式提供的回覆介面，以完成您要求的傳送。該回覆可能依上一點暫存在程序記憶體並顯示於 Android 通知系統，但不會寫入本應用程式的持久訊息歷史，也不會上傳到本應用程式的伺服器。
  • 為了顯示聊天室管理清單，本應用程式會在裝置本機保存聊天室名稱、分類、最後活躍時間、通知開關與聊天室頭貼。這些資料不包含完整訊息記錄，不會離開您的裝置。
  • 選配的聊天室開啟偵測只會即時比對目前 LINE 聊天室名稱與仍顯示的 Notify+ 通知；不會保存 LINE 畫面、訊息本文、輸入文字或點擊紀錄，也不會模擬點擊、手勢或輸入。辨識不完整或同名聊天室有歧義時會保留通知。
  • 您可透過 Android 的「清除儲存空間」或解除安裝本應用程式，刪除上述本機資料。本應用程式設定為不參與 Android App 備份；實際裝置移轉行為仍可能因 Android 或裝置廠牌而異。
  • 本應用程式不包含任何廣告、分析工具（analytics）或使用者追蹤機制。

4. 網路連線用途

本應用程式沒有 INTERNET 權限，不會自行連線到網路，亦沒有後端、分析服務或 App 內更新功能。若您透過 Google Play 安裝，應用程式更新由 Google Play 提供。

5. 權限說明

  • 通知存取：讀取 LINE 通知以提供增強功能（核心功能）。
  • 傳送通知：顯示本應用程式重新整理後的通知。
  • 無障礙服務：選配且預設關閉；只用於偵測目前開啟的 LINE 聊天室並清除對應的 Notify+ 通知。其他通知功能不需要此服務。

6. 兒童隱私

本應用程式並非以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，亦不會在知情情況下蒐集兒童的個人資料。

Notify+ Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This policy explains how Notify+ ("the App") handles your data. Please read it before use.

1. What the App Does

The App is a notification enhancement tool. It reads notifications posted by the LINE app and re-displays them in a more readable form (thread mode, grouped mode, etc.).

2. Data the App Accesses

To provide its features, the App requires Notification Access permission to read the content of LINE notifications, including sender name, message text, chat room name, and sender avatar. If you separately choose to enable the experimental LINE chat-open detection feature, Android's accessibility service lets the App access LINE screen structure and visible text; the App uses only the current chat name and necessary screen state to clear the matching notification.

3. How Data Is Used

  • All processing happens locally on your device. The App does not upload or store received notification content on developer-operated servers, an App backend, or other services. When you actively use Quick Reply, that reply is passed to LINE as described below.
  • Message text and sender information are held temporarily in process memory to rebuild notifications in real time; the App does not write a complete message history to its own persistent files. To display a notification, the content is passed to Android's notification system and may remain there according to your notification-history settings until the notification is cleared or the system retention period ends, even after the App process exits.
  • Only when you actively use Quick Reply does the App pass the reply you entered to the reply interface supplied by the LINE app, in order to perform the send action you requested. The reply may be held temporarily in process memory and shown by Android's notification system under the rule above, but it is not written to the App's persistent message history or uploaded to an App server.
  • To display the chat management list, the App stores chat names, categories, last-active times, notification switches, and chat avatars locally on your device. This data does not include a complete message history and never leaves your device.
  • The optional chat-open detection feature only compares the current LINE chat name with active Notify+ notifications in real time. It does not save LINE screens, message content, typed text, or tap history, and it does not simulate taps, gestures, or input. If detection is incomplete or chat names are ambiguous, the notification is kept.
  • You can delete this local data through Android's “Clear storage” action or by uninstalling the App. The App requests not to participate in Android App Backup; device-transfer behavior may still vary by Android version or device manufacturer.
  • The App contains no advertising, analytics, or user tracking.

4. Network Use

The App has no INTERNET permission and does not connect to the network. It has no backend, analytics service, or in-app updater. If you install it through Google Play, updates are provided by Google Play.

5. Permissions

  • Notification Access: reads LINE notifications to provide enhancement features (core functionality).
  • Post notifications: displays notifications rebuilt by the App.
  • Accessibility service: optional and off by default; used only to detect the currently open LINE chat and clear its matching Notify+ notification. Other notification features do not require this service.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
